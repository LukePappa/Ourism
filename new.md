Being is all that it is. It transcends everything and is everything. Construing it as a physical universe, one may say that it comprises spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it eventually recognizes its unity with being. Our cosmos may therefore be interpreted as an eternal and infinite act of self-realization.
