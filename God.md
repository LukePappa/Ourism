God is the transcendent source of existence. By this defininition, He is something outside the ontological order, and thus to even think of Him as a "something" is wholly innaccurate. Nevertheless, much as the intellect can recognize truth, the heart can apprehend divine love. It purifies the faculty, drawing one closer to Him.

Construing existence as a physical universe, it may be said to comprise spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it grows indefinitely in communion with God.
