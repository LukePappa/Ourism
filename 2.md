Outwardly, one in Absolute Prayer is silent, with their eyes closed and head bowed. Inwardly, they are surrendered completely to God, the eternal, omnipotent, omnipresent, omnibenevolent, and omniscient creator of the universe, who is the divine essence of existence. The divine essence is of three inseperable and uncreated natures: truth, goodness, and beauty. Union with God through Absolute Prayer is one's true purpose.
