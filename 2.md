Your purpose is to continually attune to reality, which is of one essence in threefold expression: Divine Love as truth, goodness, and beauty. Your inward state will be reflected outwardly as bodily, social, emotional, and vocational discipline.

Reality may be interpreted more derivatively as something that comprises spatiotemporally located observers who range in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems, and who are subject to the laws and principles of physics. Consequently, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it simply defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch of ever-finer attunement to reality.
