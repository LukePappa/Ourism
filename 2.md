All beings are subject to a world-branch of eventual personal annihilation in God, eternal, omnipotent, omnipresent, omnibenevolent, and omniscient source of existence, who is divine love. One may fulfill one's purpose through unceasing Absolute Prayer.

Outwardly, one in Absolute Prayer is seated, alone, and silent, with their eyes closed and head bowed. Inwardly, there is a calm and relaxed awareness of God.
