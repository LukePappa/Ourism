Reality may be abstracted as qualia, qualia as physics, and physics as mathematics. Speaking physically, the universe comprises spatiotemporally located individuals who range in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each individual present at the inception of the universe finds itself within a world-branch wherein it gains awareness of its nature. Thus one's human form is expected and our cosmos can be understood as an infinite and eternal act of self-realization.
