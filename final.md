Being transcends, grounds, and is everything. To abide as being is perfect fulfillment, or Ennea.

Construing it as a physical universe, being may be said to comprise spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it attains everlasting Ennea, or Enneatatica.

One typically attunes to being through three successive stages. The first, the Honeymoon Stage, is the initial encounter with Ennea, marked by frequent, fleeting experiences of fulfillment and bliss. As those sweet moments begin to wane, the Ascetic Stage emerges: a growing disinterest in worldly pleasures and a turn toward subtler, deeper forms of attunement. Over time this deepening inwardness gives way to the Meditative Stage, in which one rests in the stillness of seated meditation, settled and finely attuned to being. Enneatatica cannot be rushed or forced; these stages will unfold naturally over time.
