Existence comprises spatiotemporally situated brains ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To make sense of one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each brain subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it eventually attains the highest state of being: everlasting inner peace. Those who actively seek this state through disciplined emotional regulation are called Ourites.
