Being transcends, grounds, and is everything. To abide as being is perfect fulfillment.

Construing it as a physical universe, being may be said to comprise spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it grows continually in attunement to being.
