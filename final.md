Being transcends, grounds, and is everything. To abide as being is perfect fulfillment. Construing it as a physical universe, it may be said to comprise spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. 

To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it grows continually in awareness to being. 


Our cosmos may therefore be interpreted as an eternal and infinite act of self-realization.
