
Any naturalistic justification for existence must presuppose some element of what it seeks to explain. To avoid this circularity, it is necessary to posit a transcendent source: God. By this definition, God is beyond the possibility of articulation or comprehension. Nevertheless, much as the intellect sees truth, the heart surrender to His presence. The faculty is purified by His love, bringing one closer to Him.

Existence itself comprises spatiotemporally situated brains ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To make sense of one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each brain subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it continually grows in communion with God.
