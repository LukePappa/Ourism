Existence is eternal, indivisible, unchanging, complete, and boundless. Its appearance is expressible in the language of qualia, qualia in that of physics, physics in mathematics, and mathematics in logic. Speaking in those terms, the universe is an unbounded act of self-realization, where everything present at its inception perceives a world-branch in which it becomes increasingly conscious of existence. To live in accordance with your purpose, obey the single meditative instruction "Be".
