In an unbounded act of self-realization, everything present at the inception of the universe perceives a world-branch wherein it becomes increasingly conscious of direct reality, itself eternal, indivisible, unchanging, absolutely simple, complete, and infinite. The appearance of this reality may be expressed in the language of qualia, qualia in that of physics, physics in mathematics, and mathematics in logic. To live in accordance with your purpose, obey the single meditative instruction "Be".
