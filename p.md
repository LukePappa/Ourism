What actually exists is conscious experience. We abstract and formalize it into physics, which in turn gets abstracted into mathematics, and then mathematics gets reduced to logic. The true nature of consciousness is Divine Love, though it is often distorted by the misery that we impose upon ourselves in response to external events, the actions of others, and our own shortcomings. Our purpose is to attune ourselves to God by recognizing the mistake of self-imposed misery and ceasing to participate in it.
