Existence may be formalized as qualia, qualia then as physics, physics as mathematics, and mathematics as logic. Any naturalistic justification for existence must presuppose some element of what it seeks to explain. Thus, to avoid circularity, one must posit a transcendent source of existence, which may be called God. By this definition, God is neither finite nor infinite, neither simple nor complex, and neither created nor uncreated. He is something utterly beyond everything.
