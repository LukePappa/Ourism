What exists is the direct and immediate reality of Divine Love. The misery that we impose upon ourselves in response to external events, the actions of others, and personal shortcomings distorts this reality. Our purpose is to attune ourselves to God by recognizing the irrationality of self-imposed misery and ceasing to participate in it. In essence, the universe is an infinite and eternal act of self-realization, wherein it subjectively experiences ever-finer attunement to itself.
