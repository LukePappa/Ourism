The universe comprises spatiotemporally located observers who range in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which they come to attain everlasting and perfect mental clarity, or Ennea. One's progress towards Ennea may be reflected outwardly as bodily, social, emotional, and vocational discipline.
