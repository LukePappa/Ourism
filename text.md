The state in which one is free from misery is called Ennea. To attain Ennea, follow these steps:

1. Realize that all misery is self-inflicted.
2. Resolve to stop inflicting misery upon yourself.
2. When you become miserable, remember to stop inflicting misery upon yourself.

It is important to  distinguish misery from remorse, grief, mental illness, and physical pain, as these are not true suffering.
