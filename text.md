God is the transcendent source of existence. The uncreated energies of God--love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control--are made manifest to creation. Participation in these energies through contemplative prayer constitutes Theosis. The fruits of Theosis are discipline in asceticism, labor, relational conduct, and emotional regulation.

Existence can be thought of as comprising spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Consequently, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which they experience ever-deepening Theosis.
