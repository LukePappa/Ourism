The self transcends, grounds, and is immediate reality, which may be articulated as qualia, and qualia as physics. Using that language, the universe comprises spatiotemporally located individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each individual present at the instant of the Big Bang subjectively experiences a world-branch in which it comes to know the self. In this way, one's humanity is expected, while the cosmos may be understood as an infinite and eternal act of self-realization.
