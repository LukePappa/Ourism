Being itself is direct, immediate reality, prior to interpretation. It transcends any particular thing, and it is what I am. If I were to articulate being itself as physics, I could say that existence comprises spatiotemporally situated brains ranging in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems, of which I am one. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To make sense of my circumstances, I must invoke the MWI and posit that each brain subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it eventually recognizes itself for what it truly is. Our universe may therefore be interpreted as an act of self-realization.
