The self transcends, grounds, and is immediate reality, which may be articulated as qualia, and qualia as patterned activity, or physics. Using that language, the universe comprises spatiotemporally located individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that all individuals present at the instant of the Big Bang subjectively experience a world-branch in which they come to know the self. In this way, existence may be understood as an infinite and eternal act of self-realization.
