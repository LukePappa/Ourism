Being transcends, grounds, and is everything. To abide as being is perfect fulfillment, or Ennea. Construed as a physical universe, being may be said to comprise spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world branch in which it attains everlasting Ennea, or Enneatatica. Our cosmos can therefore be interpreted as an eternal and infinite act of self-realization.
