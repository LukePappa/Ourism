Experience may be interpreted as patterned activity, or physics. Speaking in those terms, the universe comprises spatiotemporally located individuals who range in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Individuals can be likened to the waves moving across water in that they retain continuity despite slowly changing form and exchanging matter over time. 

Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each individual subjectively experiences a world-branch in which it attains a "highest state of being". This ultimate form can be envisioned as complete freedom from misery, which will be termed Ennea.

To obtain Ennea, one must dismantle the integrity of all misery within their mind. This can be accomplished by internalizing this maxim: Don't impose misery upon yourself.
