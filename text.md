Being may be interpreted as qualia, which may in turn be interpreted as patterned activity, or physics. Using that terminology, the universe comprises spatiotemporally located individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that all of the individuals formed at the instant of the Big Bang are each subject to a world-branch in which they personally come to acquire a continuous awareness of being. In this way, one's humanity is rendered ordinary, unprivileged, or simply expected, while the cosmos itself becomes an infinite and eternal act of self-realization.
