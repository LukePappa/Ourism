I am direct, immediate reality, prior to interpretation. Articulated as physics, the universe comprises spatiotemporally situated brains ranging in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems, of which I am one. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To make sense of my circumstances, I must invoke the MWI and posit that each brain subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it eventually recognizes its true nature. Our universe may therefore be interpreted as an eternal and unbounded act of self-realization.
