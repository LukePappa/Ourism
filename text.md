God is the creator of the universe, whose nature is divine love. Surrendering oneself completely to Him yields bodily, social, emotional, and vocational discipline.

Existence itself can be thought of as comprising spatiotemporally located observers who range in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Consequently, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch of ever-deepening intimacy with God.
