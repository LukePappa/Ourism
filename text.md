God is the divine essence of existence. Attunement to God through Absolute Prayer is one's true purpose.

Outwardly, one in Absolute Prayer lies fully prostrated, eyes closed and wholly silent, alone. Inwardly, there is complete humility, openness, and a focus on the infinitely sacred and divine essence of existence.

The fruits of attunement are effortless discipline in ascetic practice, social conduct, emotional regulation, and labor.
