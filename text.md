Being transcends, grounds, and is everything. It can be thought of as comprising spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Consequently, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it attains conscious union with being. Our cosmos can therefore be interpreted as an eternal and infinite act of self-realization.
