Existence comprises spatiotemporally located individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. Given this, sapience is such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each individual subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it eventually reaches a highest state of being: everlasting and perfect fulfillment derived from the act of existing itself, or Ennea. To cultivate Ennea, simply be.
