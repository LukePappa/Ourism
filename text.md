The universe comprises temporo-spatially situated brains ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To make sense of one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each brain subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it experiences an ever-deepening awareness of being itself. Existence may therefore be interpreted as an eternal and unbounded act of self-realization.
