This is being. Being transcends, grounds, and is everything. 

It may be thought of as comprising spatiotemporally located observers ranging in complexity from individual particles to entire nervous systems. Sapience is consequently such a rare and significant privilege that personally gaining it defies coincidence. To explain one's circumstances, one must invoke the MWI and posit that each observer subjectively perceives the world-branch in which it becomes conscious of its unity with being. In this way, our cosmos can be seen as an infinite and eternal act of self-realization.
